"Meri zindagi mein aane ke liye aur itni khoobsurat yaadein dene ke liye... tumhara bohot bohot shukriya."

"Dil se dua hai ke tumhari nayi zindagi khushiyon se bhar jaye. Tumhe har wo khushi mile jiski tum haqdaar ho."

"Tumhari khushi se zyada mere liye kuch ahem nahi hai. Tum khush rahogi, toh mera dil bhi sukoon mein rahega."

"Zindagi kisi bhi mod par le jaye, ek baat hamesha yaad rakhna..."

"Mere darwaze aur meri baahein tumhare liye hamesha khuli hain. Jab bhi tumhe kisi madad, kisi sahare, ya bas ek dost ki zaroorat ho..."

"Main hamesha yahin hoon, tumhare saath, tumhare liye."

"Hamesha khush rehna. Khuda tumhein har buri nazar se bachaye aur tumhari jodi hamesha salamat rakhe. Ameen."
